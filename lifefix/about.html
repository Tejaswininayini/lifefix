About LifeFix

LifeFix is a practical problem-solving guide. You describe what went wrong; it matches your words to a playbook and gives you ordered steps, a checklist and warnings about when to call a professional.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Checklist progress is saved on your device only. Never enter passwords, bank or ID numbers.

Limits

LifeFix gives general guidance, not legal, medical or electrical advice. For emergencies, contact your local emergency service.
